1. Overview & Core Principles

Pulo — Stop the Scroll ("Pulo", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by SDN AI (Application ID: com.sdnai.pulo), is a digital wellbeing Android application designed to help users control social media consumption, prevent mindless scrolling, and maintain intentional app usage for Instagram and YouTube.

We are committed to clear and transparent privacy practices:

  • No Custom Account Server or User Database: Pulo does not operate a custom user server or backend database. We do not require account creation, passwords, or personal profiles stored on our own backend.
  • Firebase Analytics Integration: Pulo integrates the Google Firebase Analytics SDK to log specific custom events and collect standard usage metrics. This helps us measure feature usage, monitor app stability, and improve user experience.
  • Local On-Device Personal Storage: Detailed personal usage tracking logs, YouTube watch history and playback positions, daily pass statuses, and cached session profiles are stored locally on your device using standard Android SharedPreferences and are not sent to remote servers.

2. Screen-Time Management & Accessibility Service Usage

Pulo includes an optional Blocker Accessibility Service (BlockerAccessibilityService) to help you manage exposure to the official Instagram application.

Prominent Disclosure: Accessibility Service Usage

Why Pulo requires the Accessibility Service permission:
When explicitly enabled by the user, the Accessibility Service detects when the official Instagram application (com.instagram.android) opens a window on your device. When Instagram opens, Pulo displays a full-screen shield overlay encouraging you to use Pulo's focused messaging interface or activate one of your daily pass limits (a 5-minute or 1-minute pass). If an active pass is running, Pulo displays an overlay timer over Instagram.

Accessibility Service Technical Boundaries
  • No Content Inspection: As configured in accessibility_service_config.xml (canRetrieveWindowContent="false"), Pulo cannot and does not read, view, or extract on-screen text, images, or chat messages.
  • No Keylogging or Input Monitoring: Pulo does not monitor keypresses, typing, form inputs, or passwords.
  • No Interception of Communications: Pulo does not read or intercept chat messages, emails, or personal data.
  • Package Filtering in Code: The Accessibility Service receives window state change events from the Android system, which include the package name of the app that opened a window. Pulo immediately discards events from every app except the official Instagram app (com.instagram.android). Package names of other apps are not stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere.

B. Analytics Transmitted to Google Firebase Analytics

Pulo integrates Google Firebase Analytics (project: Pulo Analytics). When you use Pulo, certain custom app event logs and standard SDK telemetry are transmitted to Google Firebase servers.

Custom Events Implemented in Code

The following custom events are explicitly logged in the application code via AnalyticsHelper:

  • instagram_session_start: Logged when an Instagram session begins. Parameter: planned_minutes (integer representing selected session limit).
  • youtube_session_start: Logged when the YouTube screen is opened. No custom parameters are sent with this event.
  • timer_completed: Logged when a session timer expires. Parameter: timer_type (string value: "instagram_session" or "accessibility_pass").
  • protection_enabled: Logged when the Accessibility Service connects and protection becomes active. No custom parameters are sent with this event.

Automatically Collected Analytics Data

In addition to custom events logged by the app code, the Google Firebase Analytics SDK automatically collects standard device and app interaction data according to official Google documentation, including:

  • Standard app lifecycle metrics (e.g., app opens, session duration, user engagement).
  • Device and operating system details (device model, OS version, app version, language, system locale).
  • Approximate location information derived from device IP addresses by Google servers during network requests.
Clear Separation: Local Data vs. Firebase Analytics

What remains on your device vs. what goes to Firebase:
• Local Only: Specific YouTube video titles, video IDs, video playback positions, exact session time logs, pass reset dates, and cached Instagram usernames remain strictly on your local device in SharedPreferences and are never sent to Firebase.
• Sent to Firebase: Custom event names and their parameters (listed above) and standard Firebase SDK diagnostic telemetry are transmitted to Google Firebase Analytics servers.

4. Embedded WebViews & Interaction with Third-Party Platforms

Pulo embeds standard Android WebView components to provide a curated, distraction-free interface for Instagram (InstagramActivity) and YouTube (YouTubeActivity).

How WebViews Function in Pulo:

  • Direct Host Communication: When you access Instagram or YouTube within Pulo, your device connects directly to official servers owned by Meta Platforms, Inc. (instagram.com) and Google LLC (youtube.com / google.com).
  • Credentials & Authentication: When logging into Instagram or YouTube, credentials (username, password, or two-factor authentication tokens) are entered directly into official web forms. Pulo does not read, store, or transmit your credentials.
  • Cookie Management: HTTP cookies are managed directly by Android's system CookieManager to maintain web sessions. Pulo does not extract or transmit these cookies.
  • Script & Layout Customization: Pulo injects client-side JavaScript and CSS into the WebView strictly to customize page layouts (e.g., hiding recommendations, hiding algorithmic feeds, blocking endless reel swiping, and displaying navigation buttons). This code executes entirely within your local browser engine.

Your interactions with Instagram and YouTube via Pulo are governed by the Privacy Policies and Terms of Service of Meta/Instagram and Google/YouTube.

5. Android Permissions Required

Pulo requests the following Android system permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load web interfaces for Instagram and YouTube within WebViews, and transmit analytics events to Google Firebase Analytics.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the Firebase Analytics SDK to check network connectivity before sending analytics events.
  • android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE: Required for the optional Blocker Accessibility Service to detect when the native Instagram app is opened in order to display the blocker shield or timer overlay.

6. Data Control, Retention, and Deletion

You have options to manage local data stored on your device:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete individual entries from your YouTube Watch History directly from the YouTube history panel by tapping the delete icon next to any video entry.
  • Local Data Erasure: You can clear all local app logs, watch history, daily pass states, and cached web profiles at any time via Android device settings:
    Settings > Apps > Pulo > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling Pulo: Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local files, preferences, and SharedPreferences stored by Pulo on your device.
  • Analytics Data Retention: Analytics event data sent to Google Firebase Analytics is retained according to project settings configured in Google Firebase Console and Google's standard data retention policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Pulo is a general utility digital wellbeing app. It does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable digital consent age). Technical analytics collected via Google Firebase Analytics are limited to app engagement metrics and device information handled under Google's privacy standards.
